THE BIGGER PICTURE

What is SDG 11?

SDG 11 aims to make cities and human settlements inclusive, safe, resilient and sustainable.

🏙️

Sustainable Cities

Use resources responsibly and reduce the environmental impact of urban life.

♿

Inclusive Communities

Urban services and public spaces should be accessible and useful for everyone.

🛡️

Resilient Cities

Communities should be better prepared for environmental and other urban challenges.
